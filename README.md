# UNEMPLOYMENT_DATA.CSV

Average Unemployment Rate: Dataset ke mutabik, overall average unemployment rate approx 17.26% hai.

Labour Participation: Average labour participation rate 50.32% raha hai, jo workforce ki active bhagidari ko dikhata hai.

Trend Analysis: Employment numbers 1.1 lakh se 4.9 lakh tak vary kar rahe hain, jo regions ke beech economic disparities ko highlight karte hain.

Regional Disparity: Har state (Region) mein unemployment rates mein kaafi utaar-chadaav hai, jisse yeh saaf pata chalta hai ki kuch regions mein job market dusron ki tulna mein zyada unstable hai.
